#  Cottonil Supplier Performance Dashboard

Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing sales, purchases, and inventory to evaluate supplier performance.

## Business Question
Which suppliers drive purchasing, and is the company exposed to supplier dependency risk?

## Tools
Power BI · Power Query · DAX · Data Modeling

## Key Insights
- EGP 3M in sales vs. EGP 4M in purchases across 7 suppliers and 3 categories (Men, Women, Kids).
- **Supplier concentration risk:** one supplier accounts for ~85% of purchase value (EGP 3.39M of EGP 3.99M).
- Inventory of 19K units across 255 SKUs.
- Men's category drives 68.6% of purchase value.

##  Recommendation
1.Increase inventory before peak sales months such as May and June to avoid stock shortages.
2.Reduce dependency on a single supplier by expanding the supplier network. 
3.Review purchasing plans to ensure procurement aligns with actual sales demand and prevent 
excess inventory. 
4.Negotiate better pricing with high-volume suppliers to reduce purchasing costs. 
5.Focus marketing efforts on top-selling products while promoting slower-moving products to 
improve overall sales distribution. 
6.Continuously monitor monthly sales and purchasing trends to improve demand forecasting.
